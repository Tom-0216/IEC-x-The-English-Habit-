The problem

Language learning is failing Europe's children — and their parents know it.

78% of European students study English for six or more years and still can't hold a conversation. The market isn't short of options. It's short of anything that actually works.

01 / Crippling cost

£1,290–£3,440 a year

1-to-1 tutoring runs £13–£34/hour. Real fluency needs 100+ hours a year — a bill most families simply refuse to pay.

02 / Broken methods

Six years, no conversation

Grammar drills and static textbooks build no real confidence. 78% of European students still can't hold a conversation after 6+ years of study.

03 / No continuity

Gains vanish by October

Summer immersion works — then fades. No affordable, engaging bridge exists between a two-week camp and daily practice the rest of the year.

The global EFL market exceeds £150B and is growing 8% YoY — yet the quality-affordability gap for children aged 5–17 remains almost entirely unaddressed.

International study trips are coming back — this time backed by EU funding.

School-sector mobility and exchange funding of this kind runs at national level across every EU member state — exactly the kind of programme-linked funding IEC is already set up to draw on through its PON and regional grant relationships.
